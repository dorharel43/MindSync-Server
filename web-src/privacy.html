Privacy policy

Last updated: {{UPDATED}}

MindSync is a study assistant for students: it keeps your course files, tasks and calendar in one place, writes summaries and practice questions with AI, and can add your study plan to Google Calendar. This page explains what information MindSync keeps, why, and what control you have over it.

What we keep

  • Your account: email address, a securely hashed password (never the password itself), your name and degree.
  • What you put in the app: tasks, calendar events, folders, the files you upload (and the text extracted from them), summaries, practice questions and your review history.
  • Feedback you send from Settings, with your account id and browser type, so we can reply and fix problems.
  • Usage counters: how many AI actions you used each day, to apply the daily limit, and on which days you used the app, so we can see whether it's useful enough to come back to. Only the app's owner sees these numbers.
  • Server logs: technical records of requests (address and time), kept for a short time to find errors.

We do not sell your information, show ads, or use advertising or tracking cookies. The app stores your login session and display preferences in your browser's local storage.

How the AI features use your files

When you ask for a summary, practice questions or to read a syllabus, the content of that file is sent to Google's Gemini API to produce the result, which is then saved in your account. It is sent only for the action you asked for. Google processes it under the Gemini API terms.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you connect it:

  • MindSync asks for permission to create its own calendar and manage the events in it (calendar.app.created), plus your Google email address to show which account is connected.
  • MindSync creates one calendar called "MindSync" in your account and adds, changes and removes only the events you choose to sync (classes, exams, study blocks). It cannot see or change your other calendars or events.
  • The access token Google gives us is stored encrypted and is used only to update that calendar when you ask.
  • You can disconnect at any time in MindSync (Settings → Google Calendar → Disconnect), which also cancels the access with Google, or from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Events already added stay in the "MindSync" calendar until you delete that calendar in Google Calendar.

MindSync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information from your Google account is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to train AI models, and not shared with anyone except as needed to provide this feature.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored in a MongoDB Atlas database and the app runs on Render (cloud hosting). Connections to the app are encrypted (HTTPS). These providers store and process the data on our behalf only.

Deleting your information

  • You can delete any file, task, event or question in the app at any time.
  • Settings → Reset all data deletes all your tasks, calendar items, files and questions at once.
  • Settings → Delete account deletes your account and all its data right away (you confirm with your password). It also disconnects Google Calendar.

Children

MindSync is meant for students aged 16 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
