Study smarter, with a plan that knows when your exams are.

MindSync is a study assistant for university students. Upload your course files and it writes summaries and practice questions, reads exam dates from your syllabus, and plans your week so you're ready in time.

Open MindSync - it's free

Summaries and practice questions

Upload lecture slides or notes (PDF or text). The AI writes a summary and questions you can practise with.

Spaced repetition before the exam

Questions come back at the right time, and everything you need is reviewed before the exam date.

Syllabus to calendar

Upload a syllabus and MindSync finds the exams, classes and deadlines and adds them for you.

Weekly planner and tasks

Write plans the way you'd say them ("exam in statistics on Thursday at 9") - no forms to fill in.

Google Calendar (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, MindSync creates one calendar in your account called "MindSync" and adds your classes, exams and study blocks to it. It can only see and change that calendar - not your other calendars or events.

You can disconnect at any time from Settings. Details are in the privacy policy.
